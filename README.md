# ORVYN Studio Showcase

Build a premium, modern, highly polished website for ORVYN, a digital solutions studio that helps businesses build a strong and effective digital presence.



I will provide the official ORVYN logo. Use the provided logo as the source of truth for the brand identity. Do NOT redesign, replace, distort, or generate a different ORVYN logo.



CORE PURPOSE



The primary goal of this website is to make visitors immediately understand:



1. What ORVYN does

2. What services ORVYN offers

3. The quality of work ORVYN produces

4. Why a business should choose ORVYN

5. How to contact ORVYN



The website should feel like a serious, modern digital studio — not a generic freelancer portfolio.



The experience should follow:



See → Understand → Trust → Contact



The website should be visually impressive enough that the website itself demonstrates ORVYN's design and development capabilities.



---



BRAND POSITIONING



ORVYN is a digital studio focused on creating modern digital experiences for businesses.



Services include:



- Business websites

- Web applications

- E-commerce websites

- Custom digital solutions

- UI/UX design

- Branding and digital design



Do not make the website sound like a large corporation. ORVYN should feel modern, creative, capable, personal and ambitious.



Avoid generic agency language such as:

"We are passionate about innovation."



Use confident, concise and human copy.



---



DESIGN DIRECTION



Create a premium contemporary design with:



- Strong typography

- Excellent spacing

- Large visual sections

- Clean layouts

- Subtle animations

- Smooth scrolling

- Sophisticated hover interactions

- Modern cards

- High-quality project mockups

- Strong visual hierarchy

- Excellent mobile responsiveness



The design should feel similar in quality to a top-tier modern digital product studio.



Do NOT make it look like a generic SaaS dashboard or a template marketplace.



Avoid excessive gradients, excessive rounded cards, excessive glassmorphism, unnecessary illustrations, stock-photo-heavy sections, and overly complicated animations.



The design should feel intentional and editorial.



Use the ORVYN logo's visual characteristics to influence the overall design system.



Create a consistent:



- Color palette

- Typography system

- Button system

- Card system

- Spacing system

- Border/radius system

- Animation language



Do not choose colors that conflict with the supplied ORVYN logo.



---



NAVIGATION



Create a clean sticky navigation bar.



Left:

ORVYN logo



Navigation:



- Home

- Services

- Work

- Process

- About



Right:



Start a Project



On mobile, use a polished hamburger navigation.



The navigation should become slightly more compact when scrolling.



---



HERO SECTION



Create a visually striking hero.



Primary headline:



We build digital experiences that move businesses forward.



Supporting copy:



ORVYN designs and builds modern websites, web applications and digital experiences that help businesses stand out and grow.



Primary CTA:



Start a Project



Secondary CTA:



Explore Our Work



Add a visually impressive digital composition around the hero — for example, layered website interface previews, floating project cards, subtle motion, or an abstract representation of digital products.



Do not overwhelm the hero.



The headline must remain the main focal point.



Add subtle entrance animations when the page loads.



---



SERVICES SECTION



Heading:



What we do



Supporting text:



From your first digital presence to a complete custom platform, we build solutions around your business.



Create four visually distinctive service cards.



01 — Websites



Modern, responsive websites designed to give businesses a professional digital presence and turn visitors into customers.



02 — Web Applications



Custom web applications built around the way your business actually works.



03 — E-commerce



Online stores designed to make discovering, buying and managing products simple.



04 — Design & Branding



Visual identities and digital experiences that help businesses become recognizable and memorable.



Each service should have a subtle interaction on hover.



---



FEATURED WORK / PORTFOLIO



This should be one of the strongest sections of the website.



Heading:



Selected work



Supporting text:



A look at some of the digital experiences we've designed and built.



Create large portfolio project cards with impressive device/browser mockups.



Initially include placeholder projects that can easily be replaced with real ORVYN projects.



One project should be:



Elgon Star Motors



Category:

Automotive Website



Description:



A modern digital experience designed to give an automotive business a stronger online presence and make its vehicles easier for customers to explore.



Add:



View Project



Make the portfolio section visually dominant.



Use large screenshots/mockups rather than small generic cards.



The portfolio architecture should make it easy to add more projects later.



---



WHY ORVYN



Create a section explaining why businesses should work with ORVYN.



Heading:



Built around your business.



Use three or four strong points:



Designed with purpose



Every element has a reason — from the visual identity to the customer journey.



Built for real users



We create experiences that are intuitive, responsive and easy to use.



Made for your brand



We don't believe every business should look the same. Your digital presence should feel like your business.



Built to grow



We create digital foundations that can evolve as your business grows.



Keep this section visually clean and avoid excessive text.



---



PROCESS SECTION



Heading:



From idea to launch.



Create a four-step timeline.



01 — Discover



We learn about your business, your audience and what you're trying to achieve.



02 — Design



We create the visual direction and user experience.



03 — Build



We turn the design into a fast, responsive and functional digital product.



04 — Launch



We test, refine and launch your product to the world.



Use scroll-based or subtle reveal animations.



---



ABOUT ORVYN



Create a concise editorial-style About section.



Heading:



Digital, built with purpose.



Copy:



ORVYN is a digital studio focused on helping businesses build better digital experiences. We combine design, technology and strategy to create websites and digital products that don't just look good — they work.



Add a strong visual element alongside the text.



Do not make this section excessively long.



---



CTA SECTION



Create a visually powerful CTA before the footer.



Headline:



Have an idea? Let's build it.



Supporting text:



Whether you need a website, an online store or a custom digital solution, let's talk about what you're building.



Primary button:



Start a Project



Secondary option:



Chat on WhatsApp



Make this section memorable and visually distinct.



---



CONTACT SECTION



Create a clean contact form.



Fields:



- Name

- Business Name

- Email

- Phone / WhatsApp

- What do you need?

- Tell us about your project



Dropdown for:



- Website

- Web Application

- E-commerce

- Branding / Design

- Other



CTA:



Send Project Request



Also provide a WhatsApp contact option.



Make the form feel simple and approachable rather than corporate.



For now, structure the form so the backend/contact destination can easily be connected later.



Do not invent an email address or phone number. Use clearly marked placeholders where actual contact details are required.

Navigation links:

---



ANIMATIONS & INTERACTIONS



Use animations carefully.



Include:



- Smooth page transitions

- Scroll reveal animations

- Subtle text animations

- Project card hover effects

- Image scale effects

- Button hover interactions

- Navigation scroll behavior

- Smooth anchor scrolling



Animations should feel premium and fast.



Do NOT use excessive animations that slow down the website or distract from the content.



Respect "prefers-reduced-motion".



---



RESPONSIVENESS



The website must be exceptionally responsive.



Design specifically for:



- Mobile phones

- Tablets

- Laptops

- Large desktop displays



Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.



The mobile version should feel intentionally designed.



Pay particular attention to:



- Navigation

- Hero typography

- Portfolio images

- Service cards

- CTA buttons

- Contact form

- Spacing



---



TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS



Build the website using a clean modern architecture.



Use:



- React

- TypeScript

- Tailwind CSS

- Modern component architecture



Keep components reusable and organized.



Create reusable components for:



- Navbar

- Hero

- Service cards

- Project cards

- Process steps

- CTA

- Contact form

- Footer



Keep portfolio projects in structured data so new projects can easily be added later.



Optimize images and avoid unnecessary performance-heavy assets.



Ensure:



- Fast loading

- Semantic HTML

- Good accessibility

- Proper heading hierarchy

- Keyboard navigation

- Accessible buttons/forms

- SEO-friendly metadata

- Open Graph metadata

- Responsive images



---



IMPORTANT CONTENT RULES



Do not invent:



- Client testimonials

- Client logos

- Statistics

- Awards

- Number of projects

- Fake partnerships

- Fake reviews

- Fake team members

- Fake company history



ORVYN is building its portfolio, so the website should communicate confidence without making unsupported claims.



Use real projects as they are added.



---



FINAL DESIGN GOAL



The finished website should make someone who owns a business think:



"These people can build something professional for my business."



It should feel:



Modern

Confident

Creative

Minimal

Premium

Professional

Human



The website itself should be the first proof of ORVYN's capabilities.



Prioritize visual quality, typography, spacing, responsiveness and portfolio presentation over filling the page with text.



Build the complete responsive website, not just a landing-page mockup.
